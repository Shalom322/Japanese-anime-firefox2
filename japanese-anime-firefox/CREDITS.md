## Wallpapers

`chrome/images/wallpaper.jpg` and `chrome/images/wallpaper-panel.jpg`
(the second one is a darkened, tinted copy of the first)

Background artwork generated with OpenAI image-generation tools.
It is a generic night landscape (sakura branches, mountains, moon, a girl
sitting on a hill); it does not depict characters, logos or scenes from any
existing anime, game or film.

The MIT license in `LICENSE` covers the code, the CSS, the scripts and the
SVG files only. It does **not** claim exclusive copyright over the generated
artwork. Whether and how copyright applies to AI-generated images depends on
the country and may change, so use the picture at your own discretion, and
check the terms of the service that generated it if you plan commercial use.

You can replace the wallpapers with your own pictures, see the section
"Шпалери" in `README.md`.
